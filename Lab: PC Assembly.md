## Reflection
As a first-year student, the PC Assembly and Disassembly Lab Session was a very meaningful experience for me. It was my first time opening a real computer and seeing its internal components up close. With guidance from our lecturer, I learned how to handle parts like the motherboard, RAM, power supply, and storage carefully, which helped me understand how a computer works beyond theory.

Working in a group of six, we disassembled and reassembled the PC together. This hands-on activity made learning more interesting and improved my teamwork and communication skills. I also discovered that I enjoy working with hardware, as it helped build my confidence and understanding.

I am thankful to Dr. Aryati bt Bakri for this valuable opportunity. The session motivated me to further develop my technical skills in the field of technology.
