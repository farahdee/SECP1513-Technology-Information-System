## Summary
This assignment focused on identifying key issues in cafeteria crowd management and food waste at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). Through observation, it was found that 
students often struggle to find available seating during peak hours, leading to overcrowding and discomfort. At the same time, cafeteria staff face excessive food waste due to 
inaccurate demand estimation and unsold meals. To address these issues, several 
technology-based solutions were proposed, including smart food lockers, the UniFlow mobile application, AI camera systems, and smart bins with weighing scales deployed around UTM.

## Reflection
This study helped us understand how operational inefficiencies in cafeterias affect both students and staff. The seating problem impacts students’ dining experience and time 
management, while food waste contributes to financial loss and environmental harm. By analysing these problems, we realised that digital and data-driven solutions can 
significantly improve resource utilisation. The proposed systems encourage better coordination between students and cafeteria operators while promoting sustainable practices within the campus community.
