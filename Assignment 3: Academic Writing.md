## Summary
This report reflects on a talk delivered by Ts. Hj. Abdul Alim, in which he shared his professional experiences related to project management 
and the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC). The speaker discussed the importance of effective planning, clear communication, and risk management 
in ensuring project success. He also explained the stages of SDLC and highlighted how structured development processes help organisations deliver 
systems that meet user requirements, timelines, and quality standards. Real-world examples from his career were used to illustrate how 
project management principles and SDLC are applied in practice.

## Reflection
Attending this talk enhanced our understanding of how theoretical concepts of project management and SDLC are implemented in real working environments. 
The speaker’s experiences provided practical insights that are often not fully captured in textbooks, particularly in managing project constraints 
and adapting to unexpected challenges. The session helped us appreciate the importance of leadership, teamwork, and continuous monitoring throughout 
a project’s life cycle. Overall, the talk strengthened our awareness of industry expectations and the relevance of these concepts to our future careers.
