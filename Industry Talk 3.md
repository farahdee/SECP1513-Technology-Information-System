## Introduction
On 14 January 2025, I had the opportunity to attend an industry talk titled Application Developer Analyst II. The session was delivered by Ms. Qaisara Rohzan, who shared valuable insights from her professional journey in the field of data analytics and business intelligence. The talk gave me a clearer picture of how data professionals work in real industry environments and how technology supports business operations in large organizations. It was both inspiring and informative, especially for students who are preparing to enter the IT industry.

## Summary
Ms. Qaisara Rohzan began her talk by sharing her career path, starting as a data analytics intern and eventually becoming a supervisor for the Power BI team at PPG, a global materials science company. She introduced PPG’s business units, such as aerospace coatings and automotive finishes, to help us understand how data supports real products and services.

One of the most interesting parts of the talk was her use of a “restaurant and logistics” analogy to explain technical concepts like data pipelines, ETL processes, and network security. This made complex ideas easier to understand and more engaging. She also discussed real challenges in the industry, such as VPN dependency and cloud migration, showing that working in IT involves solving practical problems every day. Overall, the talk highlighted how business intelligence connects technology with business needs.

## Reflection
From this talk, I learned that being successful in the IT and data field is not only about technical skills, but also about understanding business processes and being able to solve real-world problems. Ms. Qaisara’s career journey motivated me because it showed that growth comes from experience, learning, and guidance from mentors.

The way she explained complicated topics using simple examples helped me realize that communication is just as important as coding or analyzing data. This session also made me more interested in exploring areas like data analytics and business intelligence in the future. Overall, the talk gave me confidence and a better understanding of what to expect in the professional IT environment.
