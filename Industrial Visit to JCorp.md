## Introduction
Johor Corporation (JCorp) is a Johor state government statutory body established in 1968 to support the state’s economic development. It operates in sectors such as healthcare, agribusiness, food and beverage, and property. In recent years, JCorp has adopted digital technologies like cloud computing, data analytics, and artificial intelligence (AI) to improve efficiency and decision-making.
On 7 January 2026, students from the Data Engineering course at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia visited JCorp as part of the SECP1513 Technology and Information System course. The visit aimed to expose students to real-world enterprise systems and show how computing technologies are applied in modern organizations.

## Summary
The visit showed how JCorp has moved from traditional servers to a multi-cloud Unified Platform Architecture using Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and AWS. We learned how data is transformed into valuable products using tools such as Azure Synapse, Data Factory, and vector databases for AI. Enterprise systems like SAP, Dynamics 365, Workday, and ServiceNow are integrated through APIs to support a Generative Business Intelligence (Gen-BI) ecosystem. Overall, the session strengthened our understanding of cloud, data engineering, and AI in business.

## Improvement Suggestion
Future visits could include more hands-on demonstrations, such as viewing real dashboards, data pipelines, or AI workflows. Longer sessions and more interaction with engineers would also help students better understand real industry challenges. Providing simple pre-visit materials could further improve learning effectiveness.

## Reflection
The JCorp visit helped us connect classroom theory with real-world practice. Seeing how cloud platforms, data systems, and AI are used in an actual organization made the concepts clearer and more relevant. We also learned that successful systems require both technical skills and good communication between teams. The experience motivated us to improve our knowledge in cloud computing, data engineering, and enterprise systems for our future careers.
